Watch2Earn Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Watch2Earn intends to handle personal information when users access our website or application. Our final practices will depend on the features and services actually implemented.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect account information such as an email address, profile details, support messages, app activity, reward transactions, and information needed to detect fraud or maintain security.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to operate accounts, provide video and reward features, maintain transaction records, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, protect the service, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Videos and third-party services

Videos may be provided by authorized creators or third-party services. When you interact with embedded or externally provided content, the relevant provider may collect information under its own privacy policy.

5. Firebase and service providers

If implemented, Firebase or other service providers may process information to support authentication, data storage, security, and app functionality. We will identify the services actually used and provide links to their privacy information.

6. Advertising

If advertising services such as Google AdMob are integrated, those providers may process certain information for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

7. Sharing information

We do not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be shared with service providers supporting the app, when authorized by the user, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights and security.

8. Data retention and security

We intend to retain information only as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use appropriate security measures, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your privacy choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request information about your personal data. Contact us to submit a privacy request.

10. Children's privacy

Watch2Earn is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Age restrictions and appropriate safeguards will be applied according to the services offered and applicable requirements.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our features and data practices change. We will publish the revised policy and update the date above.
